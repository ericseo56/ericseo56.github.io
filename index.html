DEX (Dextrous Hand)

Designed DEX, a wearable hand-tracking peripheral for real-time motion sensing and tactile interaction. Built the embedded electronics stack, connecting dual rotary encoders, a 9-DOF IMU, and a thumb switch to an ESP32, with motion data carried over I²C/SPI. Wrote firmware to send calibrated sensor data to a web platform as usable finger-tracking data for rehabilitation and accessibility applications.
